to-do-calendar

A desktop calendar widget for Windows that shows Google Calendar events and Microsoft To Do tasks together.

What it does

  • Shows a monthly calendar on the Windows desktop with your Google Calendar events and Microsoft To Do tasks that have due dates.
  • Lets you add, edit, complete and delete items directly from the widget. Changes are written to Google Calendar or Microsoft To Do.
  • Optionally copies To Do tasks with due dates into a calendar named "To Do" in your Google Calendar, so they are visible on your phone.

How your data is handled

The app runs entirely on your own computer. There is no server operated by the developer. Your calendar and task data are sent only between your computer and Google or Microsoft. See the Privacy Policy for details.
