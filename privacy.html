Privacy Policy

Calodu. Effective 2026-10-06.

Summary

Calodu is a desktop application that runs on your own Windows computer. The developer does not operate any server and does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your data.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (scopes calendar.events and calendar.readonly): your calendar list and events, to display them in the widget, to create, edit and delete events you choose to change, and to keep copies of your To Do tasks in a calendar named "To Do".
  • Microsoft To Do (scopes Tasks.ReadWrite, User.Read, offline_access): your task lists and tasks, to display them and to create, edit, complete and delete tasks you choose to change.

Where data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI).
  • A local cache of your events and tasks is stored only on your computer to display the widget quickly.
  • Data travels only between your computer and Google or Microsoft over HTTPS. No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calodu's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the calendar features described above, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Remove the app's access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Microsoft at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or your organization's My Apps page.
  • Delete the local folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\agenda-widget to remove all tokens and cached data from your computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
